This form is the standard intake for onboarding new email notifications for Graph Connectors. Whether your team needs connection health alerts, admin action reminders, or status updates — complete each section below with the required details, including trigger conditions, recipient information, dynamic parameters, and full email content. Our team will review your submission and work with you to ship the notification.

Template Parameters

Parameters are dynamic placeholders that get replaced with real values when the email is sent — for example, a connection name, tenant ID, or timestamp. Define each parameter your email template needs so our system can inject the correct data at delivery time.
